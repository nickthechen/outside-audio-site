A reel-to-reel sampler. Bring your own sounds.

Two empty tapes · layered chops · live pattern recording · punch-in FX · random & latch

Thread a new tape

  1. Connect one FM-1 directly by USB.
  2. Open this page in a browser with Web MIDI SysEx support, such as desktop Chrome or Edge.
  3. Connect, then install. Keep power and USB connected until restart is verified.

This replaces the running firmware. Unsaved audio and patterns are lost on restart. Saved tapes I and II are preserved; legacy tape III stays in flash but is no longer selectable. Back it up before updating; no audio recordings are included in this release.

After installation

Load your own samples in the sampler & sample uploader. SEQ + REC records quantized chops; EDIT changes steps; ENV edits tape properties. Hold OCT− for random chops; tap OCT+ to toggle rhythmic latch. To delete on the device: GLOBAL → library (first page) → K1 selects tape → REC → REC again within 3 seconds. Press GLOBAL again to cycle sample/device settings. Patterns are currently session-only.

Recovery and diagnostics

If interrupted, reconnect the FM-1 and use Install again to resume from the update loader. If writing completes but restart cannot be verified, power-cycle the FM-1 and connect again. Do not disconnect while writing. To return to stock firmware, use the official M-VAVE updater with its official firmware.
